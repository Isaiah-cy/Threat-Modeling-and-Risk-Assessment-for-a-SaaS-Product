# Threat Modeling & Risk Assessment — SaaS Student Portal

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates a structured **Threat Modeling and Cybersecurity Risk Assessment** of Halisans School of Technology's Student Portal, a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform responsible for managing student information and services.

The assessment applies the **STRIDE threat modeling methodology** to identify potential security threats affecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of student information across the portal's components, interfaces, data flows, and database.

## 🎯 Objective

The project was designed to identify potential security threats, evaluate associated risks, and develop a control-based risk treatment plan through four key stages:

- **Threat Identification:** Identifying potential threats using the STRIDE methodology.
- **Risk Assessment:** Evaluating identified threats based on likelihood and impact.
- **Control Mapping:** Mapping identified risks to relevant ISO 27001:2022 Annex A and NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 controls.
- **Risk Treatment Planning:** Documenting proposed treatment approaches and security actions in a Risk Treatment Plan Register.

## 🔍 Threat Modeling & Risk Assessment

The assessment examined potential threats to sensitive student information, including:

- Personal information
- Student registration records
- Academic information
- Authentication data

STRIDE was used to systematically identify threats across the SaaS application's architecture and data flows.

Identified threats were subsequently evaluated to establish their associated risk levels and prioritise appropriate security responses.
